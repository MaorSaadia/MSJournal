Snapshot observations remain individual points and null threshold gaps are not connected. An EOD projection supported by complete saved history remains labeled calculated, not live equity.

Requirements distinguish Met, In progress and Needs information. Its activity projection scopes original source contributions to the owned account, exact phase boundaries and effective firm-session rules. Cached content remains visible during same-account refreshes.
