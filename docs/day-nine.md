# Day 9 — Prop accounts, rules and progress

Prop Accounts extends the existing owned trading accounts. It adds account/program metadata, Paused status, dated evaluation/funded periods, custom rule versions, explicit status events, manual prop-dashboard snapshots and account-level progress. No official firm templates are supplied: Apex, MyFundedFutures, Lucid, TPT and Custom remain organizational labels. The dashboard layout and its financial calculations remain unchanged.

## Configure your first account

1. Open **Prop Accounts** and add or edit the existing account. Enter its nickname, firm, program, broker identifier, nominal size and actual account start date. Currency remains USD, matching the existing journal. Nominal size is never a balance baseline.
2. Open **Overview** and save a tracking phase. Choose the actual phase start/cutoff in the account reporting timezone, not simply today's date if you need earlier imported history. Enter an observed balance immediately before that exact cutoff, and confirm whether imported realized history since then is complete. An existing journal baseline is only a visible prefill to confirm, never an inferred nominal balance.
3. In **Rules**, choose the effective timestamp, gross/net basis and any optional target, qualifying-day requirement, daily loss rule, drawdown parameters or consistency threshold. Blank means unconfigured; zero is explicit. Confirm the IANA rule timezone, local reset and start/end-date label convention before day results are shown. Rule-day settings are independent of the journal display timezone.
4. Import **Performance.csv** into that account and configure known/estimated fees where appropriate. Orders.csv and broker APIs are not required. Account-specific import links select that eligible account rather than another account; the existing import policy still requires an Active, unarchived account.
5. Record dated observations from the firm's dashboard in Overview. Use the explicit dated status actions to mark passed/failed/paused/closed, or start a funded phase/reset. For a new broker identifier, first create its separate owned trading account and select it in the transition form.

Configuration copying loads an owned source version into the Rules form for review; it does not activate a rule. Reconfirm the target's day settings and enter its starting high-water mark, then save a dated version. Account settings cannot silently change a tracked account's phase/status; use the dated actions instead.

## What the values mean

- **Imported trading profit:** exact decimal sums of relevant matched realized exit portions since the phase cutoff. Deposits and withdrawals are excluded. Net profit remains incomplete when fees are unknown; estimated fees are labeled. Phase profit and qualifying-day progress do not reset when a chart filter or rule version changes.
- **Imported balance estimate:** the explicit phase baseline plus net realized results and recorded cash adjustments. Known fee coverage and confirmed history are required. A buffer is labeled at its last imported close, never as live available risk.
- **Manual observations:** balance, liquidation threshold and optional day count, with an as-of timestamp and note. They remain isolated observations in the chart. A snapshot changes no P&L, cash movement or baseline and does not imply compliance before its timestamp or an official threshold after it. Snapshot buffer is calculated only between the two values in that same observation.
- **Baseline confirmation/correction:** explicitly changes the original cutoff's observed baseline/history confirmation, preserves the prior value in dated event history, and does not move the cutoff. Recent snapshots are never substituted automatically.

Latest import, latest realized activity, latest snapshot and analysis timestamps are distinguished. Refresh progress after the firm's dashboard or your journal changes; this is a CSV-based journal tracker, not broker monitoring.

## Supported custom rules and limits

Profit targets use phase-to-date profit. Saved requirements apply according to their effective dates; historical versions remain available and are evaluated only through their period end. Qualifying-day counts accumulate completed supported rule days using the rules in force for each interval. Partial and unfinished days are labeled and excluded from finalized day counts. Earlier unconfirmed/unconfigured day rules can make the full-phase count incomplete. Counts represent imported realized activity rather than opening-trade coverage.

- **Static floor:** compares a supported balance estimate with the configured fixed minimum; cash affects balance, not trading profit.
- **End-of-day trailing:** uses the configured initial high-water mark and supported completed rule-day closing balances, minus trailing distance, limited by the optional floor cap. The floor is non-decreasing within a version and takes effect at the closing reset. A completed partial first day can supply a closing balance without being counted as a complete qualifying day. An unfinished current day cannot advance the floor. Cash movements within the version suspend inferred trailing floors until the user supplies a new manual observation or explicit dated high-water configuration; their effect is not guessed.
- **Intraday trailing:** equity highs cannot be reconstructed from Performance.csv. Use dated manual balance/threshold observations; no later official threshold is extrapolated.
- **Daily loss:** compares chronological realized-close prefixes with minus the configured limit, batching equal timestamps. Strictly below the boundary is an observed crossing; equality is the boundary. A recovered final total does not erase a crossing. Unrealized equity and unobserved intraday breaches remain unsupported.
- **Consistency:** largest positive qualifying-day P&L / total phase-to-date P&L, only for a positive total and supported complete day history. The denominator includes losing and nonqualifying results, excluding cash. Strict `<` and inclusive `≤` thresholds are supported. Mixed gross/net historical versions or unresolved day history leave the ratio unavailable. This is a custom formula, not a claim about every firm's policy.
- **Maximum contracts:** stored as a reference only; matched exits do not establish concurrent open exposure.

Passing visible checks never automatically marks an account Passed, Failed or payout-eligible. Missing history, fee uncertainty, ambiguous phase/rule boundaries and unsupported equity observations affect confidence. Source positions spanning a phase/reset boundary are flagged and excluded from phase attribution rather than silently assigned. Rule changes preserve phase totals; resets create a new non-overlapping period without deleting sources, reviews, screenshots or earlier configurations.
